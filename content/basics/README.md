# Lo básico (The basics) — overview

Free, **offline**, no AI needed. For learners starting from (almost) zero.
A **quick check** at the start ("¿Ya sabes lo básico?") lets learners who already know it skip to the Core.

**Format:** shorter lessons (3–4 min), mostly listening and pictures, very little reading. Each unit has 3–4 lessons and ends with a **listening game** instead of an AI role-play. Everything is taught through work examples, never "the apple is red".

| Unit | Topic | Key content | Work reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | Numbers | 0–100, then 1,000+; how numbers are said at work (*one-oh-five*, *two-fourteen*) | Aisles, doors, rooms, quantities |
| **B2** | Time & days | *seven o'clock*, *seven-thirty*, *a quarter to*, a.m./p.m., Monday–Sunday, today/tomorrow/tonight | Shifts, breaks, schedules |
| **B3** | Money | dollars & cents (*nineteen fifty*), *an hour*, *per week*, *paycheck*, *tip*, *change* | Pay, register, tips |
| **B4** | Dates & forms | months; **US dates are month first** (10/22 = October 22); *first name, last name, date of birth, signature*; form words | Applications, HR paperwork |
| **B5** | The alphabet | letter names; spelling your name; trouble letters (see below) | HR, forms, phone |
| **B6** | US measurements | pounds (lbs), ounces, feet, inches, gallons, °F; rough kilo/meter comparisons | *Can you lift 50 lbs?*, recipes, measuring |
| **B7** | Directions, colors & sizes | left/right, up/down, top/bottom shelf, front/back, next to, behind; colors; S/M/L/XL | Following instructions, finding things |
| **B8** | 20 key work verbs | take, bring, put, open, close, push, pull, lift, carry, clean, check, wait, help, call, start, stop, finish, turn on/off, move | They appear in almost every instruction |

## Notes moved here from Core Unit 2 (for B5)

**Letters that confuse Spanish speakers**
| Letter | Sounds like | Careful! |
|---|---|---|
| A | éi | not "a" |
| E | ii | sounds like Spanish **I** |
| I | ái | not "i" |
| G | yii | |
| J | yéi | |
| H | éich | |
| R | ar | |
| Y | uái | |
| W | dábel-iu | |
| Z | zii | buzzing sound, like a bee: *zzz* (US says *zee*, not *zed*) |

Planned B5 dialogue (HR): *"What's your last name?" — "López." — "How do you spell that?" — "L-O-P-E-Z." — "Is that E or I?" — "E."*
Privacy: when learners practice their own name, it stays on the phone only.
